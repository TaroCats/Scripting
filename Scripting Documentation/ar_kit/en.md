The global `ARKit` API tracks full-body motion with the rear camera and faces with the front camera on supported devices. Check `ARKit.getCapabilities()` for body tracking or `ARKit.getFaceTrackingCapabilities()` for face tracking before starting a session.

- [ARKit Body Tracking](scripting://doc?title=ARKit%20Body%20Tracking) explains session control, joint transforms, tracking quality, and the squat-counting demo.

- [ARKit Face Tracking](scripting://doc?title=ARKit%20Face%20Tracking) delivers front-camera poses, expression weights and the native face mesh.

- [ARKit World Tracking](scripting://doc?title=ARKit%20World%20Tracking) detects surfaces, tracks the camera, and supports world-space rays and anchors.
